Privacy Policy for Debt Countdown Planner

Effective date: October 26, 2026

Debt Countdown Planner is a debt payoff planning application. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information you enter

You can enter debt balances, interest rates, payment amounts, schedules, and related financial details. This information is used to calculate and display your debt payoff plans. The app is designed to store this information locally on your device rather than in a developer-operated online account.

Accounts and bank connections

The app does not require an account and does not connect to your bank or automatically retrieve bank transactions.

Purchases

The app offers an optional one-time Pro upgrade through Google Play Billing. Google processes the purchase under its own terms and privacy policy. The app may receive purchase status information needed to enable or restore Pro features. The developer does not receive your full payment card details from the app.

Advertising and analytics

The app does not display third-party advertisements. No developer-operated analytics or advertising tracking service is intended to collect the debt information you enter. Google Play and Android may independently process technical or purchase-related information under Google's policies.

Backups and sharing

If you choose to export or share an app backup, you control where that file is saved or sent. Anyone with access to an exported backup may be able to view the information it contains. Protect backup files and delete copies you no longer need.

Data retention and deletion

Debt data entered in the app remains on your device until you edit or delete it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app, subject to any backups you have made. Deleting data in the app does not automatically delete copies you exported elsewhere.

Security

We aim to minimize data collection by keeping debt planning information on your device. No storage method is completely risk-free; use your device's security features and keep backups secure.

Children

The app is intended for general financial planning and is not directed to children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the app or legal requirements. The effective date above will be updated when changes are published.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the support contact listed on the app's Google Play store listing.
